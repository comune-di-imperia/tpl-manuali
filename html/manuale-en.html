Before you start

What it is and what you need

The self-driving bus is an experimental public transport service: electric shuttles run along a dedicated route with an on-board operator always ready to take over. Taking part is voluntary and free of charge: to board you must register once and give your informed consent, as required by the decree authorising the trial.

All you need is

  • a smartphone with an internet connection;
  • an email address you can open right away (or a Google account);
  • less than a minute to complete the registration.

There is no app to install. Open your phone browser and go to app-tpl.comune.imperia.it: the page works on any phone, tablet or computer. You can add it to your home screen to find it with a single tap.

Please note. The application follows the language of your phone and is available in Italian, English, French, German and Spanish. You can also pick a different one from the language selector on the page. The buttons named in this guide are the ones you will see in English; the screenshots were taken in Italian, so the layout matches even where the wording does not.

Step by step

Registration

1Create an account

On your first visit choose Create an account. Enter your email address and a password of at least 10 characters, then press Create an account.

In a hurry? On the sign-in screen you can choose Continue with Google: no email or password is needed and you go straight to the consent step. The service only receives your address and the name Google provides, never your password.
The account creation screen.
2Confirm your email address

After submitting, a green confirmation message appears: if the address is valid you will receive an email containing the link to open.

The message comes from Self-driving bus pilot project; any replies go to tplauto@comune.imperia.it.

Nothing received? Check your junk mail folder. After 24 hours the link expires: simply request it again from the account creation screen.
The notice confirming the message was sent.
The confirmation email: open the link within 24 hours.
3Complete the confirmation

The link opens this screen: press Confirm the address to complete the check. This is the last step of the account creation.
A single tap completes the check.
4Sign in

Go back to the sign-in page: the green notice “Address confirmed” means everything is in order. Enter your email and password and press Sign in.

Forgotten your password? Use Forgotten password below the button: you will receive instructions by email to choose a new one.
Sign in with credentials or with Google.
Step by step

Consent and your data

5Read the privacy notice

The Welcome on board screen introduces the trial. Tap Privacy notice to open it: it is written in plain language and explains which data are collected, who sees them and how long they are kept.

When you have finished reading, press Continue.
The notice opens and closes with one tap.
The privacy notice, first part.
Account deletion and authorisation reference.
6Enter your details and give consent

Fill in the fields marked with an asterisk: First name, Last name, Date of birth, Sex and User category. Employment status is optional.

Finally tick the consent box and press Confirm and register.

Why these details. First name, surname and date of birth allow the operator to check, when you board, that the code really is yours. Age band, sex, relationship with the city and employment status serve the university research, which receives them without your name and without your address.
The details form and the informed consent box.
Step by step

Boarding

7Registration completed

The green tick confirms that your consent has been recorded. From now on you are a trial participant: your participation code and the registration date are shown.

Continue with Show the code to obtain your boarding code.
Registration complete.
8Show your boarding code

The QR code is what the operator scans when you board. It is regenerated every few seconds: this is normal and prevents anyone else from reusing your code.

When the code is read, your first name, surname and date of birth appear for a few seconds on the operator's device, solely to check that the code is yours.

At the stop. Open the code page shortly before boarding; turn your screen brightness up so it is read at once; do not take a screenshot of the code: after a few seconds it is no longer valid.
The boarding code, with its refresh countdown.
9Rate the service

After the ride, from the same page choose Leave a rating: tap one to five stars and, if you wish, add a comment. Rating is optional and can be skipped.

Do not write personal data in the comment, neither yours nor other people's: the text is read by those who analyse the results of the trial.
A rating and, optionally, a free-text comment.

How the trial service works

The trial is organised in progressive phases. The electric shuttles run on a dedicated route at a maximum speed of 15 km/h, with an attendant on board always able to take manual control. In the phase with passengers, up to eight seated people travel besides the attendant; the service runs on demand and rides are free of charge.

Frequently asked questions

If something does not add up

The QR code keeps changing: is that a problem?

No, that is how it is meant to work. The code is regenerated every few seconds precisely so that it cannot be copied and reused by others. Show it directly from the page, without photographing it.

I have not received the confirmation email.

Wait a few minutes and check your junk mail. Make sure you typed the address correctly. The link is valid for 24 hours: after that, simply register again.

Can I board without registering?

No. As this is a trial, taking part requires informed and voluntary consent: registration is how that consent is collected.

Do I have to pay anything?

No. Rides during the trial are free and registration is free of charge.

Do I need to download an app from the store?

No. The service is a web page: open it in your phone browser at app-tpl.comune.imperia.it.

I have changed my mind: can I withdraw my consent?

Yes, at any time, using Delete your account in the top bar. From then on you can no longer board, unless you register again.

Is my movement tracked?

No. Only the vehicle position at the moment of boarding is stored. The route you travel is not tracked.

Must I show an identity document to the operator?

When boarding, the QR code is enough: your details appear on the operator's device and are used only to check that the code is yours.
